How it works

Point it at your products once. It works overnight and hands you decisions, not busywork.

Install

One command. It checks your Mac, sets up a small local database and puts MarkOS on your Desktop.

Show it your products

Add each product and its repo folder. The team reads it and asks you to confirm what it understood. Where docs disagree, it lists both and guesses nothing.

Wake up to a brief

The night shift researches, drafts and reports, within a nightly cap that fits a Claude plan. You approve, edit or reject in the morning.

A whole team, each with a job

Each exec works from real sources, carries a playbook from the best operators in its field, and says what isn't happening.

Chief of Staff

Routes your instructions and writes the morning brief.

CMO and marketing team

Researches the live web, drafts a strategy you approve, writes content and reviews it before you see it.

CFO

Pricing, unit economics and runway, from your code and the numbers you enter. Never invents revenue.

CPO and CTO

What shipped, what to announce, where marketing over-promises, and repo risks. Repos are read only.

Sales

Design partners and customers, each with a source, and pitches drafted for you to send.

COO and your assistant

What's due, stuck or waiting on you. Your assistant books a meeting only when something needs you.

Safe by default

An agent that reads the web can be steered by what it reads. MarkOS is built so that a hostile page can't reach your files, your keys or your accounts. The first five lines are checked by tests in the repo.

PromiseChecked by
✓Agents get only the tools a step needs: web search, or read-only file tools. Never write, edit or shell tools.tests/llm/claude-code.test.ts
✓One call never gets both the web and your files, so a web page can't steer an agent into leaking a file.tests/llm/claude-code.test.ts
✓File-reading calls are denied .env*, *.pem and SSH keys.tests/llm/claude-code.test.ts
✓The app answers only this Mac. Other websites can't change anything.tests/utils/local-guard.test.ts
✓The database gives nothing to a request without the app's own key, and every table has Row Level Security on.tests/engine/db-access.test.ts
✓Nothing public happens on its own: a post goes out only after you approve it, an email only when you press Send.The product's rules, in the README

The agent tests check which tools and deny rules MarkOS asks Claude Code for on every call; Claude Code's permission system enforces them.

What you need

  • A Mac. Built and used on Apple silicon; Intel Macs should work but haven't been tried.
  • Node 22 or newer.
  • Claude Code, signed in to your Claude plan (run claude, then /login).
  • That's all. MarkOS is new and has been used day to day on one Mac. If a step fails, open an issue and we'll fix it.
